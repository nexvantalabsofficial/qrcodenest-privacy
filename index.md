# QRCodeNest — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 19 November 2026
**App:** QRCodeNest (package `com.nexvantalabs.qrcodenest`)
**Contact:** nexvantalabsofficial@gmail.com

## Summary

QRCodeNest does not collect, store on any server, upload, sell or share your personal information. Everything the app does happens on your phone. The app has no internet access at all.

## What the app does with your data

| Feature | What happens | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Scanning with the camera** | The camera is used only while the Scan screen is open, to read QR codes and barcodes. Camera images are analysed on your phone and are not saved or sent anywhere. | Nowhere. Stays on device. |
| **Scanning from a photo** | You choose an image with the Android photo picker. The app can read only the image you pick. | Nowhere. Stays on device. |
| **Creating codes** | The text, links, Wi-Fi details, contact details and other content you type are used to draw the code on your phone. | Nowhere. Stays on device. |
| **My Codes / history** | If you choose to save a code, its content and style are saved in the app's private storage. Wi-Fi passwords are encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore. | Private app storage on your phone. |
| **Exporting and sharing** | Saving or sharing a code image happens only when you tap Save or Share, through the Android system dialogs. What you send, and to which app, is your choice. | Only where you send it. |

## Permissions

- **Camera** — the only permission the app requests. It is used to scan codes while the Scan screen is open.
- The app does **not** request internet, storage, location, contacts, microphone or phone permissions.

## No tracking, ads or analytics

QRCodeNest contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and does not create any advertising ID or user profile.

## Opening what you scan

When you scan a code, the app shows you its content first. A link, phone number, email, Wi-Fi network or other action is opened, dialled, sent or joined only after you explicitly tap and confirm. Once you open a link in your browser or another app, that app's own privacy policy applies, not this one.

## Data retention and deletion

Saved codes and settings live only in the app's private storage. You can delete individual codes in the app, or remove everything by uninstalling it. Android backup is disabled for the app, so its data is not copied to cloud backups.

## Children

QRCodeNest does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change (for example, if a future version adds ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
